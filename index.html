How importing works
Download a CSV from Statements & activity on the Amex website, one per statement if you like, and pick them all at once: each purchase goes to the month its date belongs to. Purchases get a category from the shop name where it can tell (fix any under Purchases below and that shop is remembered); refunds lower the totals; payments to Amex are skipped; anything already imported is skipped too.
